How to Obtain This Cancellation by Post

1. Prepare Cover: Address a blank or commemorative postcard/envelope with appropriate postage stamp affixed.

2. Outer Envelope: Insert your prepared cover inside an outer envelope addressed to: The Postmaster, .

3. Request Letter: Include a polite request asking for the Permanent Pictorial Cancellation to be applied neatly on the stamp, and request the cover to be dispatched back to your address.

Understanding Permanent Pictorial Cancellations

What is a Permanent Pictorial Cancellation (PPC)?
A Permanent Pictorial Cancellation is a special postmark provided by India Post at designated post offices located near major historical monuments, wildlife sanctuaries, places of pilgrimage, or cultural heritage sites. Unlike regular circular date stamps, PPCs depict line diagrams or artistic representations of local landmarks.

Historical Note: Permanent pictorial cancellations were first introduced in India in 1951-1954 (e.g., Qutub Minar, Sanchi Stupa, Ajanta Caves). A major expansion phase occurred in 1974-1977.

How Collectors Obtain PPCs:

  1. In Person: Visit the designated Post Office and request the postal clerk to hand-cancel your stamped mail or cover.
  2. By Postal Request (Mailing Method):
    • Affix adequate postage onto an envelope/postcard.
    • Place it inside a larger outer envelope.
    • Address it to "The Postmaster, [Post Office Name], [District], [State] - [PIN]".
    • Enclose a short note requesting the application of the Permanent Pictorial Cancellation on the stamp.
